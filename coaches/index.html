What is a Post-paid Promo Campaign?

It means I come up with the idea, write the campaign and launch it for you.

It runs on your email list and your socials.

I work on commission only.

You pay nothing upfront. If the promo makes no sales, you pay nothing at all.

What you get.

  • The idea. A real reason for your people to buy this week.
  • The emails and social posts. All of it is written in your voice.
  • The launch. I load it into your email platform and get it live.
  • A page check. I can review your sales page before the promo starts.

Three kinds of promo.

  • Seasonal. Black Friday, Christmas, the New Year.
  • Personal. Your birthday or your years in business.
  • Business. A launch, a new bonus or an open door to your program.

We pick the one that fits your month.

How it works.

  1. 1

  2. 2
    I build the promo.I pick the idea and write the copy. You choose the product with me.
  3. 3
    You read it.That is your only job. You can say no at any time. The risk is on me.
