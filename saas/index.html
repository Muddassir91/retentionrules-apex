Where the money leaks.

  • Trials end and no sale comes.
  • Users pay, then never set up the app.
  • People sign up for a webinar, then go quiet.
  • Your list only hears from you when you launch.

You paid to get each of these people. Each one is still on your list.

How the work runs.

STEP 1

Read the chart

I read your sales calls, support tickets and customer replies. Then I check the revenue by email.

STEP 2

Find the leak

Within 48 hours, you get 3 to 5 leaks. Each one is named. The audit is free.

STEP 3

Fix it

I write the emails in your voice. We launch them. Then we check the numbers.

What gets built.

  • Welcome emails that get a new user to the first win.
  • Trial and cart emails for people who were close to buying.
  • Webinar follow-up for people who showed up and did not buy.
