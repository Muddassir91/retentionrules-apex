The two changes.

  • More sends. We went from one email a week to two or three. Unsubscribes stayed in the normal range.
  • New formats. Stories, reader questions, Reddit notes and customer reviews took turns.

How each email worked.

First came a story worth reading on its own. Then came a soft plug at the end.

The product never led. It always closed.
