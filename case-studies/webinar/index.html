How the emails ran.

  • Same day. The first email went out within hours. It took on the top doubt from the live chat.
  • Days 2 to 4. Three emails. Each one explained one feature and tied it to one pain.
  • Day 5. A customer story that matched the reader.
  • Days 6 and 7. A clear deadline and the full bonus list.

Why it earned more each year.

  • Cold traffic and the existing list each got their own follow up.
  • Each cycle, the weakest email got swapped for a new idea from the research.
  • Each email ran a subject line test. Weak ones got cut in the same campaign.
