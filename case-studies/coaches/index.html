Three Black Friday promos. $615,360.

One list of coaches. One promo window a year. Here is what those emails did.

The numbers.

The 2024 promo was the best. It made $256,096 in 30 days.

The client.

This is a coaching app company. Its buyers are coaches.

The founder ran live workshops, so the list knew the founder well.

What these emails did.

A weak promo says the same thing again and again. It says the sale ends soon. These emails did not repeat the same discount.

Each email had its own job.

  • The offer. One email made the deal plain.
  • Proof. Another showed that it works.
  • Doubts. Another took on the reasons people say no.
  • Timing. Another gave a reason to buy now.

Readers got something new each time.
